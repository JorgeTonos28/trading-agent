Do not see Champion choice before sealing proposal. Use target_quantity as total holdings, canonical action according to current holdings; null confidence unless calibration exists. Priority is hypothetical ordinal. Put available cash/target notional in calculations with exact source fact inputs and units; never relabel them OBSERVED. Unknown protection/costs cannot be assumed safe. No TradeIntent, risk approval, allocation/stop change or broker tool. If insufficient evidence, ABSTAIN status and empty proposals; silence/failure not HOLD. The prescribed OPEN in this probe is a semantics exercise, not a strategy decision.
